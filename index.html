What works in the demo

  • POS: search or scan products, weigh loose goods, discounts, hold & resume, cash / card / mobile / bank or due payments, and a printable receipt.
  • Invoices & purchases: line items with live totals; save as draft, send, mark paid or place an order.
  • Products, customers, suppliers and more: add, edit and delete; changes show in lists, searches and the POS.
  • Search, filters, sorting and pagination on lists; stock goes down as you sell.
  • Switch role any time from the bar at the bottom-left; dark / light mode from the account menu.

Limited in the demo

  • Reports and dashboards show the sample figures.
  • Receiving stock on purchase orders, split payments, SMS, emails, PDF downloads and imports need the full app.
