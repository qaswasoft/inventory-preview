Inventory — interactive demo

A click-through copy of the Laravel inventory & POS app, built from the real pages of the running app with the seeded sample data. Nothing is sent to a server: any change you make is kept only in this browser.

staff@inventory.com

 Public

Sign-in page

The login screen. Any password works here; the email picks the role.

What works in the demo

  • Every page, menu and dialog looks exactly like the real app (same compiled CSS, fonts and markup).
  • Switch role any time with the pill at the bottom-left of each page.
  • Search and filters on lists, delete confirmations, form validation, and “saving” that sticks in your browser.
  • Dark / light mode from the account menu.

What does not

  • There is no database or server, so lists don't re-sort or paginate, and new records don't appear in tables.
  • Payments, SMS, PDF downloads and imports are not wired up.
